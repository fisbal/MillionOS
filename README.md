# MillionOS

Bilingual (EN/FR) Canadian Armed Forces knowledge challenge. Enlist in the Navy, Army or Air Force, as an NCM or an officer, and climb — game after game, promotion board after promotion board — to Admiral/General or CAF Chief Warrant Officer.

**Play:** https://fisbal.github.io/MillionOS/

- 944 questions in 10 categories (Navy, Army, Air Force, Ranks, Equivalents, Drill, Inspection & dress, History, OREN lessons, Seamanship) and 4 difficulty levels
- Career mode with merit points and promotion boards; Training mode by category; Review of your mistakes (spaced repetition)
- Achievements and per-category mastery
- Official insignia and ship images from canada.ca (National Defence)
- Lifelines: Direct order (50:50), Call the watch, Crew vote, Change of watch
- 30-second timer from question 6, explanation after every answer
- 7 original music styles generated live (Naval, Pop, Gospel, R&B, Classical, Modern Arabic, Asian) and sound effects
- Installable on phone or tablet, works offline after the first visit

Unofficial practice game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Canadian Armed Forces. Questions use public information only and do not replace your course or manuals.
